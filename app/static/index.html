        Activity

        ⇩ Campaigns sheet ⇩ Activity sheet

          Email the businesses you found

          1. Search with a business type (Search tab) – e.g. Roofer in Houston, No website, Must have: Email.
          2. Check the emails of that business type (left, Business types): the first email and the follow-ups, and on which day each goes out.
          3. Create a campaign from the search – or from a lead file in the type's Google Drive folder – and look at the preview; send yourself a test.
          4. Start: the server emails the businesses in the send window, a few minutes apart, and stops the follow-ups for anyone who replies, bounces or unsubscribes.

          Nothing is ever sent before you press Start on a campaign.
